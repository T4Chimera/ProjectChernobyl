# Creator-approved expansion after Phase 1

The creator's follow-up authorizes gameplay development beyond the original
Phase-1-only boundary. The original full specification remains unchanged in
`GAME_DESIGN.md`; this document records the new scope and implementation choices.

- Six total Chernobyl sectors including every reactor area. The prototype uses
  Reactors 1–4 and unfinished Reactor 5–6 construction sectors, not six operating
  historical reactors. The layout is fictionalized and configurable.
- Facility lights are off. Every player always receives a flashlight mounted on
  their weapon. No batteries or flashlight-off control are added.
- Develop gameplay, all named characters' animation/voice systems, and multiple
  execution variants. Existing identities, health, names and dialogue remain canon.
- The creator requested at least two executions per character, supplying 20
  sequences: Felon 2, Sukhoi 3, Mikoyan 3, Nikolai 2, Novak 2, John 2, Jayden 2,
  and Katya 4. They involve character-specific firearm, axe, katana, appendage,
  striking, grabbing, throwing and knockdown choreography.
- The creator requested blood effects without extensive gore. The implementation
  uses intact-body adaptations of the injury/dismemberment references: staged
  strikes, short pins, knockdowns and cinematic cutaways with limited abstract
  blood specks. No splitting, severing, exposed wounds or detailed injury is built.
- This follow-up supplies action references, not new spoken dialogue or audio
  recordings. Keep approved lines exact, proposed lines opt-in, and silent
  characters silent. Uploaded recording/animation/model assets are still pending.

The resulting deliverable is a playable systems prototype rather than finished
historical architecture, character art, authored animation, or recorded acting.
